Beixi (Chloe) Du

I’m an M.Eng. student in Financial Engineering at Cornell University, expecting to graduate in December 2026. I work with Yumeng He and Prof. Chenfanfu Jiang in the UCLA AIVC Lab.

At Cornell, I have worked with Prof. Kilian Q. Weinberger and Prof. Sarah Dean on agent learning, document retrieval, and belief-space control. Previously, I earned my B.A. in Mathematics from McGill University, with minors in Computer Science and Economics.

I study how intelligent systems learn from demonstrations, use information, and make decisions under uncertainty. My work spans LLM agents and retrieval, belief-space learning and control, and visual reasoning evaluation.

Retriever-Free Retrieval: Navigating Documents with Decision Models

Chao Wan*, Paul Jünger*, Beixi Du, Bodong Liu, Navaneetha Krishnan, Kilian Q. Weinberger

DecisionRAG uses decision models to navigate a corpus from broad topics to individual passages. It jointly evaluates candidate branches without generating tokens, task-specific training, or a first-stage retriever.

Visual reasoning benchmarkNeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations & Datasets Track

The Prestige: Benchmarking Cognitive Visual Reasoning using Magic Tricks

Shuo Wen, Beixi Du*, Edwin Meriaux*, Junming Shi*, Chloe Si*, David Meger, Doina Precup, Gregory Dudek

A video-understanding benchmark built from 180 professional magic performances and 2,177 open-ended questions. It tests temporal and causal understanding, cognitive visual reasoning, and robustness to misleading questions, revealing a substantial gap between current models and human performance.

Dual Control of Linear Systems from Bilinear Observations with Belief Space Model Predictive Control

Daniel Cao*, Beixi Du*, Andrew Lowitt*, Sunmook Choi*, Sarah Dean, Yahya Sattar

Belief-space model predictive control plans over both state estimates and their uncertainty when control inputs affect observation quality. Experiments investigate the benefits of uncertainty-aware decisions when the separation principle fails.
